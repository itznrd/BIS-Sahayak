IS 16102 (Part 1) - Self-ballasted LED Lamps for General Lighting Services

Scope: This standard specifies requirements for self-ballasted LED lamps used for general
lighting purposes with a rated voltage of 220-240V. It covers safety requirements including
electrical insulation, thermal performance, and mechanical strength.

Certification: Products under this standard require ISI mark certification through BIS.
Manufacturers must apply for a license under the Compulsory Registration Scheme (CRS) if the
product falls under the notified list of electronics and IT goods.

Testing Requirements: Samples must be tested at a BIS-recognized laboratory for parameters
such as insulation resistance, dielectric strength, thermal cycling, and photometric output
(lumen output, color temperature). Test reports must be submitted along with the license
application.

Related Standards: IS 16101 (Self-ballasted LED lamps - Performance requirements),
IS 10322 (General lighting - Safety requirements for luminaires).

Common Queries: Consumers often ask how to verify if an LED bulb is genuinely ISI certified.
The ISI mark on the product, along with a license number, can be verified on the BIS CareNet
portal or the BIS Standard Mark App.
