BIS Hallmarking Scheme for Gold Jewellery

Scope: Hallmarking is a purity certification scheme for gold jewellery and artefacts. It is
mandatory in India for gold jewellery sold by registered jewellers, under the BIS Hallmarking
framework.

Certification Process: Jewellers must register with BIS to sell hallmarked jewellery. Each
piece is tested and marked at an Assaying and Hallmarking Centre (AHC) recognized by BIS. The
hallmark includes the BIS logo, purity grade (e.g., 22K916 for 22 karat gold), the AHC
identification mark, and a unique HUID (Hallmark Unique Identification) number.

Testing Requirements: Gold purity is verified using standard assaying methods at the AHC.
Each item is assigned a HUID which can be verified by consumers through the BIS CARE app.

Related Standards: IS 1417 (Methods for determination of gold purity), IS 2790 (Guidelines
for manufacture of 22 karat gold jewellery).

Common Queries: Consumers frequently ask how to verify a hallmark before buying. This can be
done via the BIS CARE mobile app by entering the HUID number, or by checking that all four
hallmark components (BIS logo, purity grade, AHC mark, HUID) are present.
