Overview of BIS Product Certification Schemes

ISI Mark Scheme: The traditional product certification scheme where manufacturers apply for
a license against a specific Indian Standard, undergo factory inspection, and submit samples
for testing at a BIS-recognized laboratory. Once granted, the ISI mark can be used on the
product along with the license number.

Compulsory Registration Scheme (CRS): Applies mainly to electronics and IT goods notified
under the Electronics and IT Goods (Requirements for Compulsory Registration) Order. Under
CRS, manufacturers register their product model after testing at a BIS-recognized lab, and
must display the Standard Mark with a registration number, but the process is generally
lighter than a full ISI license (no periodic factory surveillance in the same way).

Foreign Manufacturers Certification Scheme (FMCS): Allows manufacturers located outside India
to obtain a BIS license for their products, following a similar process to the ISI mark
scheme but adapted for factories located abroad.

Hallmarking Scheme: A separate certification stream specific to gold and silver jewellery,
focused on purity verification rather than safety/performance testing.

Licensing Procedure (general steps): 1) Identify the applicable Indian Standard for the
product. 2) Apply online via the BIS Care Manak portal. 3) Factory inspection by BIS
officials. 4) Sample testing at a BIS-recognized laboratory. 5) Grant of license and
authorization to use the mark, subject to periodic surveillance and renewal.
