IS 4151 - Protective Helmets for Two-Wheeler Riders

Scope: This standard covers requirements for protective helmets intended for use by riders
of two-wheeled motor vehicles, including shell construction, padding, retention system, and
visor requirements.

Certification: Helmets falling under this standard are covered by the Compulsory Registration
Scheme, and manufacturers must hold a valid BIS license to use the ISI mark. Selling helmets
without ISI certification is prohibited under the relevant government notification.

Testing Requirements: Helmets are tested at BIS-recognized labs for impact absorption,
penetration resistance, retention system strength, and peripheral vision. A minimum shell
weight and thickness must also be verified.

Related Standards: IS 4151 is often referenced alongside IS 2925 (Industrial safety helmets)
for comparison, though the two serve different use cases (road safety vs industrial safety).

Common Queries: Many consumers ask how to differentiate a genuine ISI-marked helmet from a
counterfeit. Genuine helmets carry the ISI mark, a BIS license number, and manufacturing
details molded or printed on the inner shell, not just a sticker.
